# Gemini 3 Pro Image - Prompting Guide

Comprehensive guide for crafting effective prompts with Gemini 3 Pro Image (Nano Banana Pro).

## Prompting Philosophy

**Golden Rule:** Describe the scene, don't just list keywords.

Narrative, descriptive prompts yield better images than simple keyword lists. Instead of "sunset, beach, palm trees," use "A serene beach at sunset with palm trees silhouetted against an orange sky."

## Prompts for Generating Images

### Photorealistic Scenes

Use photography terminology to achieve photorealistic results.

**Key Elements:**

- Camera specifications (lens type, focal length, aperture)
- Lighting conditions (golden hour, studio lighting, natural light)
- Level of detail (sharp focus, depth of field, bokeh)
- Photography style (portrait, landscape, macro)

**Examples:**

- "A portrait of a woman in her 30s, shot with a 85mm lens at f/1.8, soft natural window light, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on eyes, professional headshot quality"
- "Wide-angle landscape of mountain range at golden hour, shot with 24mm lens, f/11 for deep depth of field, dramatic clouds, vibrant sunset colors reflecting on lake in foreground"
- "Macro photograph of water droplets on a leaf, shot with 100mm macro lens, f/2.8, soft diffused lighting, extreme detail, bokeh background"

### Stylized Illustrations & Stickers

Specify artistic style and desired aesthetic.

**Key Elements:**

- Art style (flat design, vector art, watercolor, oil painting)
- Color palette (vibrant, pastel, monochrome)
- Line work (bold outlines, minimal lines, sketch-like)
- Background treatment (transparent, solid color, gradient)

**Examples:**

- "Cute sticker-style nano banana character, flat design, bold black outlines, vibrant yellow, simple shapes, transparent background, kawaii aesthetic"
- "Watercolor illustration of a coffee shop, soft pastel colors, loose brushstrokes, artistic splatter effects, dreamy atmosphere"
- "Vector art logo of a mountain peak, minimal geometric shapes, two-color palette (navy and gold), clean lines, modern minimalist style"

### Accurate Text in Images

Specify text content, placement, font characteristics, and integration.

**Key Elements:**

- Exact text content (in quotes)
- Font characteristics (bold, serif, handwritten, modern)
- Text placement (centered, top left, integrated into design)
- Text relationship to image (overlay, part of scene, object in scene)

**Examples:**

- "A vintage movie poster with the title 'NANO BANANA' in bold red serif font at the top, subtitle 'The Golden Adventure' in elegant script below, 1950s aesthetic"
- "A coffee shop menu board with chalk-style handwritten text listing 'Espresso $3', 'Latte $4', 'Cappuccino $4.50', rustic wooden background"
- "A motivational poster with 'STAY FOCUSED' in large bold sans-serif font centered on image, minimalist black text on white background"
- "A product label for organic honey jar with 'Pure Wildflower Honey' in elegant calligraphy, decorative bee illustrations, vintage aesthetic"

### Product Mockups & Commercial Photography

Emphasize professional presentation and commercial appeal.

**Key Elements:**

- Product positioning (centered, angled, lifestyle context)
- Lighting setup (studio lighting, soft boxes, three-point lighting)
- Background (seamless white, lifestyle setting, contextual environment)
- Camera angle (eye-level, overhead, 45-degree)
- Feature highlights (close-up details, product in use)

**Examples:**

- "Product photography of a smartwatch, centered on white seamless background, studio lighting with soft shadows, 45-degree angle showing screen and band, professional commercial quality"
- "Lifestyle product shot of coffee mug on wooden table, morning sunlight from window, steam rising from coffee, book and reading glasses nearby, cozy atmosphere"
- "Overhead flat lay of skincare products arranged on marble surface, soft natural lighting, minimalist composition, clean and bright aesthetic, Instagram-style"

### Minimalist & Negative Space Design

Leverage composition and spatial relationships.

**Key Elements:**

- Negative space description (where emptiness should be)
- Subject placement (rule of thirds, off-center, corner placement)
- Color simplicity (limited palette, monochrome, single accent color)
- Compositional balance (asymmetric, centered, weighted)

**Examples:**

- "A single red balloon in the bottom right corner against a vast white background, minimalist composition, lots of negative space, simple and clean"
- "A lone tree on the left third of the frame, right two-thirds empty pale blue sky, minimalist landscape, serene and spacious"
- "A small coffee cup centered in the lower third of image, upper two-thirds soft gradient background from cream to white, minimalist product photography"

### Sequential Art (Comic Panels / Storyboards)

Describe panel composition, character actions, dialogue, and mood.

**Key Elements:**

- Panel description (establishing shot, medium shot, close-up, POV)
- Character actions and expressions
- Dialogue and text placement (speech bubbles, captions)
- Scene mood and atmosphere
- Continuity between panels (if creating multiple)

**Examples:**

- "Four-panel comic strip: Panel 1 (wide shot) office worker at desk looking tired. Panel 2 (medium shot) coworker offers coffee with speech bubble 'Need a pick-me-up?'. Panel 3 (close-up) first worker's face brightening. Panel 4 (medium shot) both smiling with coffee, speech bubble 'Thanks!'"
- "Storyboard panel: Establishing shot of urban street at night, neon signs, rain-slicked pavement, noir atmosphere, cinematic framing"
- "Comic panel: Close-up of superhero's determined face, dramatic upward angle, speed lines in background, thought bubble 'I can do this', bold ink style"

## Prompts for Editing Images

### Adding/Removing Elements

Clearly specify what to add or remove and how it should integrate.

**Examples:**

- "Remove the person in the red shirt on the left side of the photo, fill in naturally with background"
- "Add a rainbow in the sky above the mountain, natural and realistic appearance"
- "Remove all text and watermarks from this image, preserve the underlying content"

### Inpainting/Masking

Describe the region to edit and the desired result.

**Examples:**

- "Change the color of the car in the center from red to blue, maintain all reflections and highlights"
- "Replace the sky in the upper half with a dramatic sunset, blend naturally with the horizon"
- "Remove the speaker overlay box in the top right corner, extend the background slide content"

### Style Transfer

Specify target style while preserving composition or content.

**Examples:**

- "Transform this photograph into a watercolor painting, maintain the composition, soft and artistic"
- "Apply the artistic style of the reference image to this photo, preserve the subject and composition"
- "Convert this realistic portrait into a cartoon illustration, bold outlines, simplified colors"

### Advanced Composition

Combine elements from multiple images cohesively.

**Examples:**

- "Blend these five people into a group photo standing side by side, natural lighting, consistent perspective"
- "Combine the foreground subject from this image with the background from the reference, seamless integration"
- "Create a product mockup placing this logo onto the reference product image, realistic and natural"

### High-Fidelity Detail Preservation

Specify critical features that must remain accurate.

**Examples:**

- "Change the background to a beach scene, preserve the person's facial features exactly, maintain sharp detail"
- "Adjust lighting to nighttime, keep all architectural details and textures intact, preserve building character"
- "Add artistic effects but preserve the legibility of all text in the image, maintain brand consistency"

## Resolution and Aspect Ratio Considerations

### Resolution Selection

- **1K (default):** Fast generation, good for iteration and testing
- **2K:** Higher quality, professional use
- **4K:** Maximum quality, studio-grade output, highest cost

### Common Aspect Ratios

- **1:1** - Social media posts, profile pictures, thumbnails
- **16:9** - Presentations, video thumbnails, widescreen displays
- **9:16** - Mobile screens, Instagram Stories, vertical video
- **4:3** - Traditional displays, print media
- **3:2** - Standard photography, DSLR cameras
- **21:9** - Ultra-wide cinematic shots

## Multi-Image Prompting

When using multiple reference images, be explicit about the role of each image.

**Examples:**

- "Create a scene combining these characters (first 3 images) in this environment (last image), consistent lighting"
- "Generate a new image in the style of the first reference, with the composition of the second reference"
- "Office photo of these five people, maintain facial resemblance for each person, professional setting"

## Google Search Grounding

Use `--search` flag when prompts reference real-time or factual information.

**When to Use:**

- Weather conditions ("today's weather in San Francisco")
- Sports scores and statistics
- Current events
- Factual data (scientific diagrams, historical maps)
- Real-world objects with specific appearances

**Examples:**

- "Create an infographic about the String of Turtles houseplant focusing on its origins, care requirements, and growth patterns" `--search`
- "Generate a weather visualization for Seattle showing today's forecast" `--search`
- "Create a diagram of the solar system with accurate planet sizes and positions" `--search`
